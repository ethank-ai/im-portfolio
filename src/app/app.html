A note from the studio

There is something quietly radical about making things slowly. About choosing the knot over the shortcut, the natural fibre over the synthetic, the object that gets better with time.

Illusional Macrame is a one-woman studio creating tactile pieces for thoughtful spaces. No two are ever quite the same, and that is exactly the point.
